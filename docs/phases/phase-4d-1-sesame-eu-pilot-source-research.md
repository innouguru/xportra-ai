## Purpose and evidence labels

This document records an initial official-source inventory and identifies
what is and is not established by the retrievals made on the research date.
It is not legal advice, a complete export checklist, a determination that a
rule applies to every sesame shipment, or evidence of government approval.

Use these labels throughout:

- **Verified source fact:** A statement visible in retrieved official source
  content, limited to the source's actual scope.
- **Research lead:** A claim or pointer supplied for investigation that was
  not independently confirmed in the retrieved source content.
- **Candidate requirement:** A source-backed matter requiring qualified
  human review, exact classification and applicability checks, and formal
  approval before it may become authoritative in the exporter catalogue.
- **Unresolved:** Information not established by the sources retrieved here.

Retrieval date for accessible source pages was 2026-10-20. A page's retrieval
date is not its publication or last-updated date. No source is asserted to be
complete or current beyond what the retrieved content itself establishes.

## Proposed pilot description

The planning direction is raw/unprocessed sesame seed (*Sesamum indicum*)
intended for human consumption, sourced operationally in Kano and Jigawa,
Nigeria, for consignments intended for Dutch import and customs clearance.
This does not establish that seeds from these states have special or
different regulatory conditions, that the actual consignment meets the
intended-use description, or that a particular preparation has a given
Combined Nomenclature (CN)/TARIC or Nigerian export classification.

The source inventory is too incomplete to support a defensible published
checklist. The NEPC pages remain inaccessible and the NAQS PDF text was not
read. Authentic EU Official Journal text and the identified amendments now
support category 3 verification of the listed sesame row and relevant
general controls; they do not establish category 4 shipment applicability.
The product owner later agreed for planning on food-use, raw/unprocessed
sesame and intended Dutch import/customs clearance; staff-upload-only intake
was also agreed as the direction, with exact formats and PDF support pending.
Those decisions do not establish product classification, legal applicability,
route/BCP, complete source coverage, or publication. See the
[Phase 4D.2 decision record](./phase-4d-2-sesame-eu-pilot-product-owner-decisions.md)
and [Phase 4D.4 readiness assessment](./phase-4d-4-pilot-scope-and-implementation-readiness.md).

### 5. European Union — Regulation (EU) 2019/1793 consolidated text

- **Authority:** European Commission.
- **Title:** Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1793 of
  22 October 2019 on the temporary increase of official controls and
  emergency measures governing the entry into the Union of certain goods
  from certain third countries.
- **URL:** [EUR-Lex consolidated text, CELEX 02019R1793-20260630](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02019R1793-20260630)
- **Official alternative representation:** [Publications Office consolidated XHTML, CELEX 02019R1793-20260630](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/9ac7df3e-6f76-11f1-ae88-01aa75ed71a1.0008.02/DOC_1)
- **Jurisdiction:** European Union.
- **Source type / format:** EU legal instrument; the alternative is the
  Publications Office's consolidated XHTML documentation text, not an
  authentic Official Journal version.
- **Retrieval / verification:** 2026-10-20. The EUR-Lex endpoint did not
  return readable text. The official Publications Office alternative was
  fetched and its text searched. It identifies itself as
  `02019R1793 — EN — 30.06.2026 — 015.001`, with a converter-generated date
  of 2026-07-07. Its disclaimer says the consolidation is a documentation
  tool with no legal effect and that authentic versions are those published
  in the Official Journal.
- **Verified text in that consolidation:** Annex II, point 1, row 12 lists
  Nigeria (NG), *Sesamum* seeds for food, CN code `1207 40 90`, and the
  specified products under `ex 2008 19 19` and `ex 2008 19 99` (TARIC
  subdivision `49` for those `ex` codes). The row names *Salmonella* as the
  hazard and gives `50` in the column headed “Frequency of identity and
  physical checks (%)”. This confirms the earlier 50% claim as a statement
  in this dated official consolidation, not as a legal-status or
  shipment-specific determination.
- **Supporting provisions in the retrieved consolidation:**
  - Article 7 provides that Annex II consignments enter the Union under the
    conditions in that section and are identified by the listed CN/TARIC
    codes.
  - Article 8(1) applies the listed frequency to identity and physical
    checks, including sampling and laboratory analyses. Article 8(2) says
    the frequency is an overall frequency for all products in the entry;
    it is not a Salmonella-positive rate and is not separately stated for
    each code in row 12.
  - Article 3(e) and Annex II footnote (21) refer Salmonella sampling and
    analysis to Annex III; the footnote specifies point 1(a).
  - Article 10(1) requires each Annex II consignment to be accompanied by
    sampling and analysis results from the competent authority of the
    country of origin, or the country of dispatch when different.
    Article 10(2)(d) specifies that for Annex II food listed due to
    Salmonella risk, those results ascertain the absence of Salmonella in
    25 g.
  - Article 11(1) says each Annex II consignment must be accompanied by an
    official certificate using the model in Annex IV. Article 4 also
    requires a duly finalised Common Health Entry Document for release for
    free circulation.
- **What it supports:** A strong, source-backed **candidate** for enhanced
  official controls and accompanying documentation when the product is
  food within the listed CN/TARIC scope and the provision is in force for
  the consignment. The row's `(Food)` designation does not establish
  applicability to sesame seed for sowing or decide the tariff
  classification of a particular processed product.
- **Legal status:** The underlying Regulation is an EU legal instrument,
  but this retrieved consolidation expressly has no legal effect. A later
  research pass verified the replacement Annex II row against the authentic
  Official Journal text of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1206. That
  confirms the row's text and effective date from 2026-06-30; it does not by
  itself establish that no later measure changed the rule by 2026-10-20 or
  confirm applicability to a particular shipment. Articles 4, 8, 10, and 11
  and their amendment/corrigendum history have not all been checked against
  authentic readable Official Journal text.
- **Freshness / completeness limitations:** The version is dated
  2026-06-30. Official Publications Office metadata identifies
  Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1441 and two corrigenda;
  the amendment text and corrections have not been fully read and compared
  with the relevant provisions. No later change was found in the limited
  records retrieved, but an exhaustive check through 2026-10-20 has not
  been established. Verify later changes, exact product presentation and
  customs classification, and applicable Member State entry conditions
  before publication.
- **Certificate caution:** The retrieved consolidation does contain an
  official-certificate provision: Article 11(1) and Annex IV apply to
  consignments listed in Annex II. This is not carried forward merely from
  older amendment wording. The claim remains verified only in the
  identified consolidation, not against the full authentic amendment chain;
  it remains unpublished until a qualified reviewer verifies the current
  text, applicability, and certificate model.

## PDF parser interface check

The regulatory `ArtifactParsingService` in
`xportra/domain/ingestion.py` accepts a `content_type` and `raw_content`
(`str | bytes`) and currently allows `text/plain`, `text/markdown`, and
`application/json`; it rejects PDF as an unsupported artifact type. Its
output is a normalized document representation used by the regulatory
pipeline.

The separate exporter evidence-upload parser in
`xportra/domain/evidence_upload.py` has a PDF branch, but its current
`_extract_pdf_text` implementation uses custom standard-library byte/string
inspection. It is not the regulatory parser interface and is not evidence
of robust PDF extraction. `pyproject.toml` does not declare PyMuPDF. The
technology baseline and ADR-0002 name PyMuPDF as a pipeline technology,
but that does not mean it is installed or integrated for regulatory
artifacts.

Therefore PyMuPDF remains a **proposed** implementation for the PDF
research/pilot input, not an implemented capability. A separately authorized
implementation task must first verify the regulatory parser and normalization
contracts, safe PDF validation/limits, encrypted and malformed PDFs,
scanned-document behavior, page-level provenance, output limits, dependency
policy, and tests. This research made no code, dependency, or interface
changes.
